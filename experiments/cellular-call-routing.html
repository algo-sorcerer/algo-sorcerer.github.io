The 8-Billion Phone Mystery: Why Does a Call Ring in Your Pocket Only?

Out of 8+ billion people on Earth, when someone dials your 10-digit number, only your mobile phone rings. No continuous GPS tracking, no satellite drain, and zero global spam broadcasts. Explore the multi-tier cellular grid, core carrier databases (HLR & VLR), cryptographic SIM pseudonyms (TMSI), and radio paging channel.

📡 Hexagonal Cellular Grid

Earth is divided into hexagonal cells served by directional radio towers (eNodeB / gNodeB). Adjacent cells reuse orthogonal frequency bands to eliminate co-channel interference ($S/I$).

🔐 MSISDN vs IMSI vs TMSI

Your phone number (MSISDN) is a public alias. Your secret hardware ID is the 15-digit IMSI on SIM silicon. Over the airwaves, towers assign a rotating temporary TMSI pseudonym to defeat IMSI sniffers.

🗄️ Two-Tier Core Registries

The HLR (Home Location Register) is the carrier's permanent master database pointing to your current regional VLR (Visitor Location Register). The VLR issues a temporary MSRN trunk route.

⚡ Radio Paging Channel (PCH)

Towers broadcast your active TMSI on a shared downlink channel. 50,000 listening phones inspect the packet, see a mismatch, and sleep. Only your phone matches, acknowledges with RACH, and rings!
